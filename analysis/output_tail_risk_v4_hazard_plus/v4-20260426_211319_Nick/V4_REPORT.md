## EV / Breakeven Analysis

- `bets_per_target_hit` = break-even payoff multiple. If a target catch pays more
  than this many normal spins of value, the policy is profitable.
- Lower is better. Compare to your real-game observations: an ACC triple typically
  pays 40-100x normal spin value; an SPN triple pays free-spin bonus which can be 30-80x.
